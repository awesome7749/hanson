# Ventrix content sources

Reviewed September 13, 2026. The owner confirmed that Ventrix Supply is Hanson Home’s sister business. The public site is https://ventrixsupply.com/.

## Used in the homeowner website

- Ventrix homepage: identifies its Woburn location, authorized TCL distribution and technical resources. Hanson’s homepage and About page now explain the sister-store connection without promising inventory, turnaround, discounts or shared warranty liability.
- TCL Apex brochure: https://ventrixsupply.com/TCL%20Products/Apex%20Multi-Zone%20Spec%20Sheet%202026.pdf . Supports the model family’s heating operating range, inverter technology and equipment-specific limited warranty summary.
- TCL Ultimate 25 brochure: https://ventrixsupply.com/TCL%20Products/Ultimate%2025%20Series%20TCL_ULT_050525_1.pdf . Supports up to 25 SEER2, connected controls and model-dependent performance. The website asks visitors to review the exact equipment combination during planning.
- Warranty center: https://ventrixsupply.com/warranty . Shows registration/claim features and requires sign-in. Linked as a Ventrix resource, not a live integration or Hanson customer portal.

## Resolve before making more detailed promises

Ventrix’s warranty page says registration within 90 days of installation. The linked Apex and Ultimate brochures instead state 60 days. The website therefore describes qualifying registration and directs customers to the applicable equipment certificate, without publishing a universal deadline.

Transfer terms also conflict: the warranty center describes transfer for a fee, while https://ventrixsupply.com/is-tcl-a-good-hvac-brand says non-transferable. That article describes automatic registration, which has not been established for Hanson projects. No transfer or automatic-registration promise was added.

Ventrix’s Apex marketing summary says full capacity at 5°F, while the brochure’s capacity table varies by model and combination. The Hanson page uses the supported operating range with a clear explanation that operating temperature is different from delivered heating capacity. No blanket full-capacity claim was added.

Manufacturer equipment coverage is separate from Hanson installation labor. Hanson labor duration, maintenance offerings and direct public contact details still require owner confirmation. No Ventrix contact information has been relabeled as Hanson’s.

Mass Save program explanations retain their official program sources. Ventrix stock, contractor pricing, incentives and service turnaround are not installation-price or eligibility guarantees.
